## 📚 Course Structure

### ✅ Course 1 — Supervised Machine Learning: Regression & Classification

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Linear Regression, Cost Function, Gradient Descent |
| Week 2 | Multiple Features, Vectorization, Feature Scaling, Polynomial Regression |
| Week 3 | Classification, Logistic Regression, Sigmoid, Decision Boundary, Overfitting, Regularization |

---

### ✅ Course 2 — Advanced Learning Algorithms

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Neural Networks, Neurons & Layers, Forward Propagation, TensorFlow |
| Week 2 | Activation Functions (ReLU, Sigmoid, Softmax), Multiclass, Backpropagation, Adam |
| Week 3 | Bias & Variance, Decision Trees, Measuring Purity, Regression Trees |
| Week 4 | Trees & Ensembles, Random Forest, XGBoost |

---

### ✅ Course 3 — Unsupervised Learning, Recommenders, Reinforcement Learning
